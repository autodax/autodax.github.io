Assumptions & Methodology

Federal 2026: $32,200 MFJ standard deduction; QBI deduction calculated at 20% of net qualified business/rental profit (subject to taxable income limits). SE tax computes 15.3% on 92.35% of Schedule C profit with half deductible. NIIT applies 3.8% above $250,000 MFJ MAGI.
California 2026: Standard deduction is $11,412. CA does not offer preferential rates for long-term gains or qualified dividends (taxed as ordinary income). Includes the 1% Mental Health Services surcharge above $1M taxable income and the $307 joint exemption credit. CA does not recognize the Federal QBI deduction.
