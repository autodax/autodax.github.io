Assumptions & methodology

2026 IRS figures (Rev. Proc. 2025-32): $16,100 single standard deduction; seven ordinary brackets (10/12/22/24/32/35/37%). Qualified dividends & LTCG stack on top of ordinary taxable income at 0/15/20%; short-term gains taxed as ordinary income. NIIT's $200,000 MAGI threshold is fixed by statute, not inflation-adjusted. "65 or older" adds the $2,050 age-65 deduction plus the temporary senior bonus deduction (up to $6,000, phased out 6%/dollar above $75,000 MAGI, gone by $175,000; 2025–2028 only). Federal-only estimate — excludes payroll tax, state tax, and credits.

How this is calculated

This splits the same taxes shown elsewhere on the page, so the rows add up to the total. Ordinary tax is layered through the brackets from the bottom: wages first, then taxable Social Security, ordinary dividends, short-term gains, and the IRA withdrawal / Roth conversion last, so it carries the highest ordinary bracket rate. The standard deduction is applied to the lowest layers first. Qualified dividends and long-term gains are taxed at 0/15/20% on top of ordinary taxable income, and the 3.8% NIIT is shared across dividends and gains in proportion to each. The employer estimate is tax on wages alone with the standard deduction; your actual withholding depends on your W-4, pay frequency, and bonuses.

How this is calculated

Standard 2026 Part B premium is $202.90/month with no IRMAA. Part D surcharge is paid on top of your plan's own premium, direct to Medicare. It's a cliff — one dollar over moves the whole year to the next tier. Uses the 2024 MAGI entered at left.

How this is calculated

IRMAA for 2028 is set by your 2026 MAGI, which here is your total income above (AGI, including the taxable part of Social Security; tax-exempt interest isn't tracked). Medicare hasn't published 2028 thresholds or premiums yet, so this uses the 2026 tier table and premiums as a stand-in. Thresholds are normally indexed upward each year, so the actual tier may be the same or lower. Not included in the 2026 tax totals.
