How it works: year 1 withdraws your initial rate times your portfolio. Each later year the withdrawal rises with inflation, except it skips that raise after a losing year when your current rate is above the initial rate. If your current withdrawal rate climbs past the upper guardrail, spending is cut by the adjustment size (no cuts in the final years); if it falls below the lower guardrail, spending is raised. Guyton-Klinger is applied to portfolio withdrawals, and Social Security is added on top (enter it after tax). Withdrawals happen at the start of each year; returns are constant at your assumed rate unless you use the stress test. The tax rate applies to withdrawals. This is an estimate, not financial advice.
